# Living Plot product baseline

> updated 2026-08-16 · 0.0.0

## Audience
Mobile entertainment users who enjoy short drama/story sessions and want to influence what happens next without writing a full story.

## Product promise
Living Plot generates personalized 60–90 second drama episodes. Every episode ends with exactly three meaningful choices, and the selected choice must create a visible consequence in later episodes.

## Phase 1 value test
The product succeeds only if users voluntarily request or return for the next episode because they want to see the consequence of their choice.

## Monetization baseline
- Free: 3 text episodes/day and 1 fresh voiced episode/day.
- Plus hypothesis: $8.99/month local-equivalent where practical, 20 text episodes/day and 10 fresh voiced episodes/day.
- No unlimited AI promise.

## Phase 1 exclusions
Generated video, voice cloning, Vector DB, multiplayer, creator marketplace, ads in the core loop, studio API, and Web/PWA product client are out of scope.
